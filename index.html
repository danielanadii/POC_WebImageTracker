HOW IT WORKS

See the house in AR

  1. 01

    Open the brochure

    Use the brochure button to view it full screen on another device or print it.

  2. 02

    Launch AR

    Tap Launch AR on your phone and allow camera access when your browser asks.

  3. 03

    Scan the brochure

    Point your camera at the full brochure and keep the house image in view.

  4. 04

    Explore the model

    The 3D home appears over the brochure. Pinch with two fingers to resize it.
